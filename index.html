05 · Hamari kahaani

10 August – Ek Yaadgar Din ❤️

Hamari Pehli Mulaqat : Rakshabandhan

Rakshabandhan ka din tha — ek khaas din, lekin mere liye us saal kuch aur hi khaas hone wala tha. Sweety aur maine decide kiya tha ki hum iss din milenge. Usne kaha tha ki wo apni mummy ke saath Manaksas aayegi. Mujhe usse milne ki itni excitement thi ki main raat bhar chain se so bhi nahi paaya.

Subah jaldi uth kar main college ke bahane 9:00 wali bus pakad li. Dil mein sirf ek hi baat chal rahi thi — aaj usse milna hai. Rahul ko bhi call karke bula liya, par main usse pehle hi pahunch gaya tha aur uska wait kar raha tha.

Kuch der baad Sweety ka call aaya — “Main pahunch gayi.” Main turant uske paas gaya, aur dekha wo apni mummy ke saath ek shop par kuch le rahi thi. Jaise hi usne mujhe dekha, ek pyari si smile di… aur us ek muskurahat ne mera poora din bana diya.

Uske samaan lene ke baad main uske peeche chalne laga, phir aage nikal gaya — mummy ke saamne kuch kehne ki himmat nahi ho rahi thi, par mann chahta tha ki thodi si baatein ho jaayein. Dheere-dheere hum bus stand tak pahunch gaye. Bus thodi der se aayi, par jab aayi to humne decide kiya ki ek saath baithenge. Uski mummy ko alag seat par baitha diya aur hum dono peeche ek saath baith gaye.

Bus chali — aur mere dil ki dhadkanein badh gayi. Hum dono haste rahe, Kurkure khaye, chhoti-chhoti baatein ki. Kabhi uski mummy peeche mudkar daant deti to hum dono chup ho jaate, phir aankhon se hi muskurate.

Mujhe uska haath pakadne ka mann tha — par himmat… nahi ho rahi thi. Phir last time par ek idea aaya — maine kaha, “Apna haath to dikha, tera chhota hai ya mera?” 😅 Aur haath milane ke bahaane… maine uska haath pakda. Usne kuch nahi kaha, sirf halki si smile di. Us ek pal ne sab kuch keh diya tha.

Phir usne bhi mera haath thoda aur tight pakda, jaise keh rahi ho — “Chhodna mat.” Us waqt meri heartbeat itni fast thi ki lag raha tha poori duniya ruk jaaye, sirf hum dono chalein.

Jab bus hamare ghar ke paas aa rahi thi to bahut bura feel ho raha tha. Mera dil bilkul nahi chahta tha ki main usse chhod kar jaun. Mann hua ki uske saath hi last stop Ghudhaa tak chala jaun. Waise to main hamesha bus mein travel karta rehta hoon, lekin wo din mere liye bahut khaas tha; sab naya-naya sa lag raha tha aur main bas aise hi uske saath chalte rehna chahta tha. Lekin samjha liya khud ko — phir bhi jab haath chhodne ka waqt aaya, to usne bhi mera haath tightly pakda rakha tha… jaise uska bhi dil nahi maan raha ho.

Wo safar sirf kuch kilometre ka tha, lekin mere liye wo zindagi ka sabse khoobsurat safar ban gaya. Us din maine samjha — milna chhoti si baat hoti hai, lekin sahi insaan se milna poori duniya jeet lene jaisa hota hai. ❤️

06 · Ek aur yaad

10 August – Chhat Wali Baat aur Tablet Ka Safar 💗

Waise to wo mere ghar ke paas hi aayi thi, lekin bus se utarne ke baad hum dono alag-alag ho gaye, taaki kisi ko zyada pata na chale aur uski mummy kisi ko kuch bata na dein.

Dil chahta tha ek baar ghar jaa kar mil loon, par himmat nahi thi. Lekin hum dono ne Instagram par baat karni start kar di. Thodi der baad hamari call par baat hone lagi. Uski awaaz sunte hi lag raha tha jaise wo mere bilkul paas ho.

Main saamne wali dungri par chala gaya, jahan se uske ghar ki chhat saaf-saaf dikh rahi thi. Usne mujhe poochha ki tum kahan ho, to phone par baat karte hue maine usse kaha, “Tu chhat par aa jaa, main yahin se dikh jaunga.” Mere kehne ke baad wo chhat par aa gayi.

Us moment ko shayad main kabhi nahi bhool sakta. Wo ek pal, ek nazar — jaise sab kuch wahi ruk gaya ho. Hum dono call par baat kar rahe the, lekin us waqt sab shabd kam pad rahe the… dil se dil tak baat pahunch rahi thi. Kabhi wo haste hue kehti — “Tu wahan par kyun gaya?” Aur main bas muskura kar kehta, “Yahan se tu sabse khoobsurat lag rahi hai.” ❤️

Phir usne halki si awaaz mein kaha, “Bus se utarne ke baad se sar mein dard ho raha hai, ek tablet la de.” Uske ye batane par main turant gaon ke medical store chala gaya. Wahan se tablet le aaya, par ab problem thi — use kaise doon? Darr lag raha tha, agar kisi ne dekh liya to kya sochega?

Main unke ghar ke peeche se gaya, socha chupke se de doon, par himmat nahi hui. Phir decide kiya — “Tu mandir ke paas aa jaana, main wahan tablet de dunga.” Wo aayi bhi, lekin uski tabiyat kharab ho gayi — ulti jaisi feeling hui, to wo wahan se wapas andar chali gayi.

Lekin mujhe jaise-taise karke tablet deni thi, to main uske ghar pahunch gaya. Andar gaya to dekha — wo kursi par baithi thi, paas mein uski mummy thi, saamne uski mami khana bana rahi thi, aur peeche uske mama ji baithe the — jo mere uncle lagte hain. Jab maine uski mummy ko dekha, to dil zor se dhadakne laga ki ye mujhe pehchaan na le. Main seedha unke peeche se gaya aur uncle ke paas baith gaya, aur bas idhar-udhar ki baatein karne laga — sirf isliye taaki sab normal lage.

Lekin andar se bas ek hi baat chal rahi thi — “Kaash ek baar uske paas jaa kar ye tablet de paaun.” Par nahi de paaya… bas uski taraf dekhte reh gaya. Aakhir maine ek idea socha — main chupke se ghar ke bahar wali tanki ke paas gaya aur tablet wahan rakh di. Phir maine use message karke kaha, “Tablet tanki ke paas rakha hai, le lena.”

Thodi der baad uska message aaya — “Mil gaya 😊 … Mummy ne tujhe pehchaan liya tha 😅” Uska ye sunte hi ki mummy ne pehchaan liya, to dil se hasi bhi aayi aur thoda darr bhi laga. Par sabse zyada sukoon tha — ye jaankar ki use tablet mil gayi aur wo theek hai.

Us pal laga jaise maine koi bada kaam kar diya ho, jaise koi chhoti si jang jeet li ho. Par bas ek baat mann mein chubhti rahi — “Itna paas hokar bhi main use seedha tablet nahi de paaya, wo mere baare mein kya soch rahi hogi…” Par dil ke kisi kone se awaaz aayi — “Wo sab samajhti hai… bina kahe hi sab samajh jaati hai.” ❤️
